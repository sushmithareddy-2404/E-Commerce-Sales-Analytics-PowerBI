# E-Commerce Sales Analytics Dashboard

An interactive **3-page Power BI dashboard** built to analyze e-commerce sales performance, customer behavior, product performance, profitability, and regional trends.

The goal of this project was to turn raw sales data into an easy-to-understand dashboard that can help identify important business trends and areas that need attention.

## Project Overview

This project analyzes e-commerce data from different perspectives:

- Overall business performance
- Customer performance and profitability
- Product and category performance
- Regional sales
- Customer segments
- Top-performing customers and products

The dashboard was designed with a consistent **black and orange theme** and includes interactive navigation between the three pages.

---

## Objectives

The main objectives of this project were to:

- Monitor overall sales and profit performance
- Understand monthly sales trends
- Identify the most valuable customers
- Analyze repeat customer behavior
- Find top-performing products and sub-categories
- Compare sales across regions and customer segments
- Analyze customer profitability
- Present the analysis through an interactive Power BI dashboard

#  Key Insights

Some of the main insights identified from the dashboard include:

- Total sales are approximately **1.57M**.
- Total profit is approximately **175.26K**.
- The overall displayed profit margin is **11.19%**.
- The **Consumer segment** contributes the largest share of sales.
- **West** is the strongest region in the displayed regional comparison.
- **Office Supplies** is the highest-sales category.
- **Phones** are the leading sub-category in the Top 10 Sub-Categories analysis.
- **Caroline Jumper** appears as the top customer by sales.
- **Tamara Chand** appears as the top customer by profit.
- **3D Systems Cube Printer** appears as the leading product in the Top 10 Products analysis.

# Dashboard Features

### Interactive Navigation

A Page Navigator allows users to move easily between:

**Executive Dashboard → Customer Analytics → Product Analytics**

### KPI Cards

Important business metrics are displayed at the top of each page for quick understanding.

### Top 10 Analysis

Top-performing customers, products and sub-categories can be identified quickly.

### Visual Analysis

The dashboard uses:

- Bar charts
- Line charts
- Donut charts
- KPI cards
- Tables

to make the data easier to understand.

---

# Business Recommendations

Based on the patterns shown in the dashboard:

- Focus on retaining repeat customers and high-value customer segments.
- Give more attention to high-performing products and sub-categories when planning promotions.
- Investigate lower-performing regions and categories for potential improvement.
- Consider customer profit margin along with sales when evaluating customer value.
- Use monthly sales trends to support future campaign and resource planning.
